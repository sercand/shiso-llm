Problem: A train leaves City A at 9:00 AM traveling toward City B at a constant speed of 60 miles per hour. A second train leaves City B at 10:00 AM traveling toward City A at a constant speed of 80 miles per hour. The distance between the two cities is 340 miles. At what time do the two trains meet, and how far from City A is the meeting point?

Solution:

Step 1: Find how far the first train travels before the second train starts.
The first train travels alone from 9:00 AM to 10:00 AM, which is 1 hour.
Distance = speed × time = 60 × 1 = 60 miles.

Step 2: Find the remaining distance between the trains at 10:00 AM.
Remaining distance = 340 − 60 = 280 miles.

Step 3: Find the closing speed.
Since the trains travel toward each other, their speeds add.
Closing speed = 60 + 80 = 140 miles per hour.

Step 4: Find the time until they meet after 10:00 AM.
Time = remaining distance ÷ closing speed = 280 ÷ 140 = 2 hours.

Step 5: Find the meeting time.
10:00 AM + 2 hours = 12:00 PM.

Step 6: Find the distance from City A.
The first train has traveled for a total of 1 + 2 = 3 hours.
Distance from City A = 60 × 3 = 180 miles.

Check: The second train has traveled for 2 hours at 80 miles per hour, so it covered 80 × 2 = 160 miles. Together, 180 + 160 = 340 miles, which is the full distance between the cities. The answer is consistent.

Answer: The two trains meet at 12:00 PM, 180 miles from City A.

Problem: A rectangle has a perimeter of 54 centimeters. Its length is 3 centimeters more than twice its width. Find the area of the rectangle.

Solution:

Step 1: Let the width be w centimeters. Then the length is 2w + 3 centimeters.

Step 2: Write the perimeter equation.
Perimeter = 2 × (length + width) = 2 × (2w + 3 + w) = 2 × (3w + 3) = 6w + 6.

Step 3: Solve for w.
6w + 6 = 54
6w = 48
w = 8

Step 4: Find the length.
Length = 2 × 8 + 3 = 19 centimeters.

Step 5: Find the area.
Area = length × width = 19 × 8 = 152 square centimeters.
